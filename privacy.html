Privacy.

Last updated · 1 June 2026

What we collect

When you place an order we collect your name, delivery address, phone number, email, and payment method. When you browse, we collect anonymous usage data via cookies — page views, session length, what you added to cart.

How we use it

To fulfill your order, process payments, ship to you, send order updates, and answer support requests. We use anonymous analytics to improve the site.

Who we share it with

Payment processors (Stripe), shipping partners (USPS, FedEx, UPS), and email/SMS providers — only what's necessary to deliver your order. We do not sell your data.

Cookies

We use essential cookies (cart, login session) and analytics cookies (Plausible). No third-party advertising trackers.

Your rights

Email privacy@budgetthrills.shop any time to access, correct, or delete your data. We respond within 14 days. We comply with the CCPA and applicable US federal and state data privacy laws.

Data retention

Order records: 7 years (tax compliance). Marketing consent: until you unsubscribe. Anonymous analytics: 26 months.

Changes

If we materially change this policy we'll email everyone on our list and post a notice on the homepage for 14 days.
