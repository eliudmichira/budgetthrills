How to measure
  1. 01Stand on a sheet of paper with your heel against a wall.
  2. 02Mark the longest toe on the paper.
  3. 03Measure heel-to-mark in centimeters.
  4. 04Match to the CM column on the chart.
  5. 05Between sizes? Go up.

A note on fit:

Nike and Adidas running shoes (Pegasus, Air Max, Revolution) run true to standard US sizing. Order your regular running size.

Vans and classic low-top sneakers are true to size with standard width.

If you have wider feet or are between sizes, we recommend going up a half size for maximum comfort.
